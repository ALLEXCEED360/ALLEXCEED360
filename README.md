## 🌟 About Me

I'm a **multidisciplinary technologist** working at the intersection of **software engineering, AI, game development, and visual storytelling** — a recent CSE graduate from **BRAC University**.

I build systems *and* tell stories around them. One week I'm designing a retrieval pipeline that has to survive a benchmark, the next I'm shipping a multiplayer game on Steam or cutting a film. My work spans code, cinematography, and everything in between.

- 🔭 Currently building **EchoLens** — multimodal video intelligence with benchmarked retrieval
- ⚙️ And **OMNIPLAY** — unifying a gaming library across Steam, Xbox and PlayStation
- 🎮 Shipped **DragonDrop** — a multiplayer party game, live on Steam
- 🌱 Always learning — latest rabbit holes: GPU inference on consumer hardware & evaluation-driven ML
- 💬 Ask me about: retrieval pipelines, computer vision, Unreal Engine, or why filmmaking makes you a better engineer
